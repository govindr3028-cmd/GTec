Transit Guide

How to Reach Our Campus

Conveniently situated in central Tripunithura with quick multimodal transit links.

By Kochi Metro

Get down at the Tripunithura Terminal Metro Station. It is just a 5-minute auto ride or a pleasant 10-minute walk to Statue Junction.

By Local Bus

Buses running between Ernakulam South / Vyttila / Kakkanad and Tripunithura stop right at Statue Junction or the nearby Municipal Stand.

By Indian Railways

Tripunithura Railway Station (TRTR) is located within 1.2 km from our center, making daily travel effortless for commuters from Kottayam / Aluva.

Parking Facilities

Dedicated two-wheeler parking and convenient nearby four-wheeler parking spaces available around Statue Junction and Layam Ground.
