From humble beginnings to an international network of 500+ centres, G-TEC is synonymous with trust, quality vocational coaching, and career outcomes.

Our Legacy

Transforming Aspirations Into Rewarding Careers

G-TEC Group of Institutions was established with the vision of bridging the digital divide by delivering high-quality, practical computer education accessible to everyone. Over the last quarter of a century, G-TEC has grown into an international education powerhouse with learning centres across India, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, and Singapore.

Our Tripunithura Campus at Statue Junction was established to serve the vibrant student and working community of the historic royal town and surrounding regions including Maradu, Hill Palace, Eroor, and Udayamperoor. We offer state-of-the-art computer labs, certified industry faculty, and immediate placement links to Kochi's burgeoning IT hubs like Infopark and SmartCity Kakkanad.
